About Our Institution

Welcome to Gyanodaya Public School

Nurturing Minds. Building Futures.

Gyanodaya Public School, Katra, Muzaffarpur, is presented as a learning community committed to helping students grow academically, socially, creatively, and personally.

Our Vision

To create an environment where every student is encouraged to learn, explore, think independently, and grow with confidence.

Our Mission

To support meaningful learning through strong academic foundations, positive values, creativity, discipline, and holistic development.

Message from the Director

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world. At Gyanodaya Public School, we focus not only on academic excellence but also on fostering values, discipline, and emotional growth. Our mission is to empower students to build a brighter future for themselves and the global community."

RAMPHAL MAHTO

Director, Gyanodaya Public School

Message from the Principal
